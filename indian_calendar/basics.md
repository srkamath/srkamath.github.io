
# Basics 
* Indian aka Hindu calendar uses Lunisolar calendar which combines Lunar months and Solar years
  * The lunar cycle determines the duration of each month
  * The solar cycle determines the duration of each year 
* The lunar month is the duration from the new moon day to the next. It is made up of two fortnights (each called a paksha)
  * Shukla Paksha: new moon to full moon: waxing phase 
  * Krishna Paksha: full moon to new moon: waning phase
