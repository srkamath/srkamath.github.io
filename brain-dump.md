re: India's population exceeds China (April 2023)

UN and similar organizations have been making doomsday predictions about India's population since the 90s - making it sound like the country has a careless attitude about population growth 🙂.  I think it is quite the opposite. In fact, we should be proud of how the last few generations have progressively lowered children per family without need for any draconian measures. There may be a few segments that aren't convinced yet - I won't deny it.

Think about this - the US has a population of 350M in less than 250 years of its modern existence. India's geography and climate is far more habitable and has had a continuous and sizeable civilization for at least 2500 years. Also, India did not face decimation during World Wars or Black Death unlike Europe. Further, Europe also established settlements in North America, South America and Australia. Given that population growth is exponential, is it any surprise that we have a population over a billion in the 21st century? All in all comparing the raw population numbers of individual countries does not paint the full picture. 

My hunch is that population growth will be a non issue in India in a couple of generations - if access to education and upliftment to middle class continues at current pace. 

Access to stable and well paying jobs is a universal problem though - as globalization, automation and AI take over. The meme warfare on social media between newer and older generations in the US highlights that. India has scaled and innovated quite well historically on this front (Amul, Lijjat, ...) whereas US is doing a rather poor job on this front. Whether India can pull it off again - remains to be seen. 

Also, crowding is practically limited to big cities, if the government helps ensure the people rural areas have viable employment opportunities, and if we reverse globalization to prefer local products first, I would wager that even a billion people can live quite comfortably in India 😉
